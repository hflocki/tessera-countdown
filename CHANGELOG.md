# Changelog

## 1.3.1
- The pictures come from this version's release instead of the main branch, so a screen shows the pictures of the
  version it runs.
- An input_datetime with a time of day shows that time on the day itself ("Today 18:00").
- The date's hint says that a yearly 29 February falls on 1 March in other years.
- README: the boards it was tried on.

## 1.3.0
- **Count in** years and months ("18 years and 3 months"), for a day far ahead such as retirement.
- Two new pictures: a hammock between palms at sunset, for holidays or retirement, and a camper van on the road.

## 1.2.1
- Picture as background: the words stand on the dark veil itself, readable in the light look too, and the veil stays
  dark further to the right.
- On boards without pictures, an empty date line no longer reaches below the tile.

## 1.2.0
- New option **Count in**: days (as before), weeks and days ("5 weeks and 1 day"), working days (Monday to Friday) or
  the Fridays until the day.

## 1.1.0
- New switch **Automatic** (on by default): it counts to the nearest of Christmas, New Year's Eve and Easter and moves
  on by itself. Switched off, the tile counts to what **Count down to** names: one holiday, a date of your own, or a
  calendar or entity. A tile of 1.0.0 that counts to one holiday, a date or a calendar: switch Automatic off.
- New option **Arrangement**: the picture as the tile's background with the number on a dark veil at its left (the new
  default), the number at the left and the picture to its right, or the picture as a band on top with the number
  under it.
- The picture now reaches the tile's edges, and the pictures are wider (600 x 400), their subject right of the middle.

## 1.0.0
- First version: the days until Christmas, New Year's Eve, Easter, the first Sunday of Advent, Halloween, the start of
  summer, a date of your own, or the next event of a calendar or the date of an entity.
- **Next holiday** (the default) counts to the nearest of Christmas, New Year's Eve and Easter and moves on by itself.
- A picture of the holiday or the season beside the number, from 2 x 1 or 1 x 2 on; on the day, the holiday's wish.
